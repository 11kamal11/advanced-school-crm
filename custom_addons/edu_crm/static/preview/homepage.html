Why Meridian

We believe a school
should leave a mark.

Not the mark of a grade — the mark of a person who knows how to think.

I  —

Academic rigour without anxiety

Our curriculum is demanding — deliberately so. But demand without support creates fear, not learning. Every teacher here uses high expectations and genuine care in the same sentence.

II  —

Small enough to know every name

1,200 students. 84 faculty. Class sizes cap at 28, so a child who is struggling is noticed in September, not discovered in March. We treat that ratio as a commitment, not a selling point.

III  —

Parents as partners, not spectators

Attendance, exam results, and fee status are available to parents the moment they exist — not at the end-of-term report. No surprises in either direction.

IV  —

A campus built for the long game

Sports, debate, music, and community service aren't extracurricular here — they're the curriculum's other half. The student who wins a science competition and plays for the school team is not an exception. That's the plan.
